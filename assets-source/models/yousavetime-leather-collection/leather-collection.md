## Provenance

Downloaded as GLB from Sketchfab (original format was FBX). Archived here as
`leather-collection.src.glb`. Exports as a single `leather-collection.glb`
containing both the couch and the armchair.

## Modifications

- Imported to `.blend`
- Removed extra nodes and renamed groups
- Scaled to real-world sizes in meters (couch width 2.2 m)
- Recombined the couch and armchair under one collection (this is the asset that
  exercises the multi-item-per-GLB path)
- Added collection export settings with copyright info
